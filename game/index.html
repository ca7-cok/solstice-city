HOW TO PLAY

Walk to the ◆ marker and press E to start a job. Steal any parked car with E. Crime raises your wanted stars — the Solstice PD sends foot cops first, then cruisers. Hide out for a while and the heat cools off. Finish all three jobs to clear your debts.
